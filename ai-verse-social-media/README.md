# AI-Verse Social Media

**Give your AI assistant a video. Tell it where and when to post. It helps prepare the video, write the words, publish it and keep track.**

You can ask for one video now or set up a regular posting routine. Use your own words—there are no special sentences to memorize.

## Remember four jobs

1. **Prepare:** make the video fit and write captions in your style.
2. **Choose:** pick the accounts and posting times, or review a draft first.
3. **Publish:** send approved work now or schedule it for later.
4. **Remember:** check each account's result, organize finished videos and avoid duplicate posts during recovery.

## Start small

Ask **“Help me set this up.”** Connect your chosen accounts, give it one video and ask for a draft. Review it before choosing where and when to post.

**[Read the simple feature guide →](START-HERE.md)** Each feature explains when it helps, what happens and something you could ask.

## What you need

An AI app that can run this skill, your Zernio account to connect your social accounts, and access to your actual videos. Google Drive is optional. Installation requires Python 3.11+, FFmpeg and private storage. Regular posting also needs your host's working scheduler and your approved rules.
