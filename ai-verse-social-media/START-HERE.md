# Your video posting helper

**Give your AI assistant a video. Tell it where and when to post. It helps with the preparation, posting and keeping track.**

Use your own words. These examples are ideas, not commands to memorize.

## Remember four jobs

**Prepare → Choose → Publish → Remember**

You choose the result. The assistant follows your preferences and asks for missing choices.

## What you can ask it to do

### 1. Help me start

It checks what is missing and guides you through setup. You connect Zernio—the service linking your social accounts—and optionally Google Drive for storing videos.

**Try:** “Help me set this up.”

### 2. Use my video

Give it a video in chat, a file on your computer, or an accessible video in your connected Drive. Sending a video alone does not ask it to publish.

**Try:** “Use this video. Show me a draft first.”

### 3. Make it fit

Trim the video, change its shape, add borders, your supplied logo or subtitles, and make the sound level more even. Different accounts can use different versions. Your original stays safe.

**Try:** “Trim the beginning and add my logo.”

### 4. Write in my style

The caption is the text beside the video. Start with your idea, exact words, the video's speech, or a mix. It adapts the text for each platform and remembers your examples and corrections.

**Try:** “Make it friendlier. No emojis.”

### 5. Use what I say

It can turn speech into written text using an installed speech tool, or use text you supply. This helps write captions based on the video and prepare subtitles.

**Try:** “Write the caption from what I say here.”

### 6. Pick the accounts

Choose one account, several, or all the accounts you included during setup. You can have several accounts on the same platform.

**Try:** “Post this on my business Instagram only.”

### 7. Pick the time

Publish now, schedule later, or save a draft to review. It can suggest suitable times within your posting rules, using account history when available. Better timing cannot guarantee more views.

**Try:** “Schedule this for tomorrow.”

### 8. Follow a routine

Set up regular work: collect ready Drive videos, choose eligible ones, prepare them and post. You choose the accounts, frequency and review rules. Your AI app needs a working scheduler to run this.

**Try:** “Post from this folder every weekday, after my review.”

### 9. Show what happened

It keeps a posting checklist for each account and checks that posts actually went out. You can see drafts, scheduled posts, published results and links when available. Successful accounts stay recorded during recovery.

**Try:** “Where has this video been posted?”

### 10. Organize finished videos

A video is filed as finished after all accounts required for its distribution confirm publication. Drive originals can move to your chosen Posted folder. Saving chat/computer videos to Drive is optional.

**Try:** “Keep my finished videos organized in Drive.”

### 11. Use old videos somewhere new

After you include a new account and allow reuse, older videos in your reusable collection can become candidates for it. Previously successful accounts are not posted to again.

**Try:** “Use my reusable videos on this new account too.”

You choose which videos join that collection. Posting a video once does not automatically add it to your regular routine.

### 12. Change plans or fix problems

Pause new sending, cancel scheduled work, publish an existing draft, investigate failures or retry a confirmed failed post. Backups save your videos, settings and posting history for recovery.

**Try:** “Instagram worked. Help finish the other account.”

Pause does not cancel posts already scheduled with the posting service. Cancellation cannot undo a published post or guarantee stopping one already being sent.

## Start with one video

1. Ask for help setting up.
2. Choose your accounts and writing style.
3. Give it a video and review a draft.
4. Tell it where and when to post.

Add regular posting when that works for you.

## What needs setup

Your AI app needs access to your accounts, videos and installed video tools. Editing covers the specific changes above. Website scraping is outside this package's core features. Want help with comments or DMs? Onboarding can collect your reply rules and check a supported Zernio/host integration. Those replies need a separately configured and tested route; the local video engine does not run them.
